Privacy Policy

GeoStamp Camera (com.mps.geostamp) · Effective 29 October 2026

GeoStamp Camera ("the app") is a camera that stamps photos with the date, time, GPS location, compass direction and address. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. We do not run any servers for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect your photos or location on our systems.

1. Information the app uses

  • Camera. Used to take photos. Photos are saved on your device in Pictures/GeoStamp.
  • Location (precise and approximate). Used to write the coordinates, altitude, accuracy, speed and address onto your photos, into the photo's EXIF data, and into the PDF, CSV and KML files you create. Location is read only while the app is open.
  • Motion and compass sensors (magnetometer, accelerometer, gyroscope). Used on the device to compute the compass heading and tilt. Sensor data is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Text you enter (for example a tag or note) and your settings. These are stored on your device only.

2. Where your data goes

  • On your device. Photos, stamps and exports (PDF, CSV, KML) are stored locally. Exports are also saved to Downloads/GeoStamp.
  • When you share. Files leave your device only when you choose to share them through Android's share sheet. The app you share to handles them under its own policy.
  • Address lookup. To turn coordinates into an address, the app uses Android's built-in Geocoder. On most devices this sends your coordinates to the device's location/geocoding provider (typically Google) and is governed by that provider's privacy policy. If it is unavailable or offline, the photo is stamped without an address.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not receive your photos, location or exports.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the gallery and photo-details screens, and an interstitial ad after you export a PDF, CSV or KML file. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use device and usage information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technologies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and ads are not requested without consent. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Permissions

  • CAMERA – take photos.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION – stamp location.
  • INTERNET – address lookup and ads.
  • VIBRATE – feedback when capturing.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below only) – save photos and exports.

You can revoke permissions at any time in Android settings; features that need them will stop working.

5. Retention and deletion

Everything the app creates stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete photos in the app's gallery, delete exported files from Downloads/GeoStamp, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot retrieve or delete it for you. Data already shared with other apps, or processed by Google (see sections 2 and 3), is subject to those parties' policies.

6. Photos contain location

Stamped photos, and the EXIF data inside them, show where and when they were taken. Think before sharing them publicly, and respect other people's privacy when photographing them.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do, and the current version is always available at this page.
